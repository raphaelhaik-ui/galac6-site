Bring Galac6 to your country: vote for your school system

Galac6 is an AI tutoring platform for pupils from primary school to the end of secondary school, with a dedicated space for teachers and school leaders. Today, it is built on the French national curriculum: the app is in French, and its interface is also available in English. We will adapt the platform (curriculum, language, dictations) to the countries that ask for it most.

How the vote works

Your school's request decides where Galac6 goes next

Step 1

You vote for your country

Fill in the short form below: your school, your country, why you are asking and what you are looking for in Galac6. One request per school is enough.

Step 2

We count the requests, country by country

Every request is read by our team and added to the count for its country.

Step 3

We adapt Galac6 to the most requested countries

The countries with the most requests come first. We get back to you when work starts on your country.

What adapting Galac6 means

Galac6, built for your pupils and your teachers

Your national curriculum

Subjects, year groups and topics aligned with the programme your school follows.

Your language of instruction

The interface and the AI tutor in the language your pupils learn in.

Dictations and texts

Dictations and reading extracts suited to your language and to each level.
