For teachers and school leaders

Your data and your pupils' data

What Galac6 does with data, in plain words. This page summarises our privacy policy (in French), which remains the reference text.

Updated on 9 November 2026

Privacy & security

Our commitments for pupils' data.

  • Data hosted in France
  • Artificial intelligence processed in Europe
  • Two-factor authentication on administration accounts
  • Your data is never sold or used for advertising
  • A parent's consent is required for children under 15, outside school settings
  • Only the pupil's teachers and the leadership of their school see their work
  • Data deleted automatically after 2 years of inactivity

Learn more (in French)

The essentials

Four points to remember.

Data hosted in France

Accounts and data are hosted in France, in a database in Paris, with our service provider Supabase.

Artificial intelligence processed in Europe

Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in Europe. Only a few technical services, such as the hosting of the website, use service providers located outside the European Union, governed by standard contractual clauses.

No advertising, no resale

No advertising in the Galac6 application. Pupil data is never sold and is not used to train artificial intelligence models.

Each person sees what concerns them

Teachers see their classes' results, not pupils' conversations with the tutor. Parents stay in control of their child's account.

What data Galac6 collects

  • Identity data: email address, pupil's name, year of birth, school level and language.
  • School activity data: messages exchanged with the tutor, quiz results, dictations, learning statistics.
  • School: name, city and country of the pupil's school. This information is used only for internal statistics; it is neither resold nor passed on to schools.
  • Display preferences: theme and text size, for example.

Who sees what

  • Teachers see their classes: the list of their pupils, their results in homework and practice, and each pupil's record. A pupil's full record is reserved for their own teachers. Teachers see neither pupils' conversations with the tutor nor families' settings.
  • School leadership sees the classes of its school.
  • Parents see their child's class and teachers. They can detach their child from the class at any time; the school and the teacher are then informed by email.

Where the data is, and who is involved

Accounts and data are hosted in France, with Supabase (database and accounts), in its Paris region.

To run the tutor, marking and voice features, Galac6 uses specialised service providers. Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in data centres located in the European Union:

  • Claude (Anthropic), for the tutor, marking and teachers' tools, through Amazon Bedrock (Frankfurt), Google Cloud Vertex AI and the Vercel AI Gateway: processing in Europe;
  • Google Cloud, for the tutor's voice, dictations and past papers read aloud, through its European endpoint: processing in Europe;
  • Mistral AI (Voxtral), for voice recognition through the microphone, through its European endpoint: processing in Europe;
  • Google Gemini (Vertex AI), for drawing the avatar created from a photo (optional): processing in Europe;
  • Brevo (a French company), for the application's emails and login emails: processing in the European Union (OVH in France and Google Cloud in Belgium);
  • Tally (a Belgian company), for optional feedback questionnaires: data stored in the European Union (Belgium); some of its technical service providers may be located outside the EU;
  • Fallback solutions: if the main AI does not respond, the functions that process pupils' work switch to fallback models that are also served in Europe (since 22 October 2026).

A few services remain located outside the European Union or are provided by American companies: the hosting of the website and application (Netlify), the gateway to the AI (Vercel, which enforces European data centres) and the payment of families' subscriptions (Stripe). Also remaining outside the European Union: the drawing of the theme's background image, from the description given by the pupil (Google Gemini), and functions that process no pupil data, such as the generation of practice papers and the reading of public official past papers.

These transfers outside the European Union are governed by standard contractual clauses, in accordance with Article 46 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The full list of our processors appears in our privacy policy (in French).

Security

  • Exchanges are encrypted (HTTPS), and papers are stored in private spaces, never public ones.
  • Access rules are applied on the server side, for each table in the database.
  • Administration accounts of the Galac6 platform are protected by two-factor authentication.
  • Passwords that have already appeared in a data leak are refused.
  • Pupils' names are progressively masked before being sent to the AI.
  • Marks proposed by the AI remain indicative: the teacher always validates.
  • If a pupil writes a worrying message, an adult at the school is alerted.
  • Photos of dictations on paper and of answer keys uploaded by teachers are deleted at the end of each school year.

The school stays in control: it decides who joins its classes, its teachers validate each piece of homework and each mark, and Galac6 acts only on its behalf.

Pupils who are minors

Galac6 is intended for pupils from CP to Terminale (grades 1 to 12). Outside school settings, for pupils under 15, the consent of a parent or legal representative is required; it is obtained when the account is created. For an account created within a school, the framework defined by the school applies.

How long data is kept

  • Pupil's account and work: as long as the account is used. An account with no login for 2 years is deleted, after a warning by email 30 days before.
  • Account deleted on request: erased within 30 days.
  • Paper copies scanned by a teacher and messages between teachers and parents: deleted with the pupil's account, and at the latest 2 years after the last message.
  • Usage statistics: 13 months.
  • Error logs: 12 months.
  • Proof of parental consent: 5 years after the end of the account.

Your rights, and those of your pupils

Everyone can ask to access their data, to correct it, to delete it, to receive it in a structured format, or object to its processing. Consent that has been given can be withdrawn at any time.

To exercise these rights or for any question about data, write to us at hello@galac6.io. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Before using Galac6 with a class

To use Galac6 with a real class, please speak to your school leadership first. To discover the teacher area, you can start alone with a demonstration class, with no real pupils. School leaders who would like to know more can write to us: we will reply to them directly.
