Galac6 and the CNED: two different roles

Galac6 is not a distance-learning school: it does not replace schooling and does not award diplomas. If your child is not enrolled at a French school, official distance learning from the French Ministry of Education is provided by the CNED.

Galac6 is a complement, whatever your child's schooling: to understand lessons, practise, revise for tests and prepare for exams.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions

Does Galac6 follow the French curriculum, even from abroad?

Yes. Galac6 follows the official French curriculum, from CP to Terminale, whatever country you live in. It does not follow any other country's curriculum.

Does Galac6 replace the CNED or a school?

No. Galac6 is a complement: it helps your child understand, practise and revise. Official distance learning, with enrolment and follow-up by teachers, is the role of the CNED.

Can my child prepare for the brevet or bac from abroad?

Yes. They practise on official brevet, French bac and maths bac past papers, including 112 set outside France, as mock exams with detailed marking. Galac6 does not handle exam registration.

My child goes to a local school. Can Galac6 help them keep up their French?

Yes. Dictations read aloud and marked, homework help in French and quizzes help them keep strong written French. The content follows the levels of the French curriculum.

How much does Galac6 cost?
