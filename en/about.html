About Galac6

Engineer, entrepreneur, and above all father of 3 children, I created Galac6 from a simple observation: homework takes up a huge part of family life — and it is a source of stress, both for children and parents.

Like you, I saw the temptation of ChatGPT: fast, available at all hours, capable of explaining everything. But also ill-suited to school, and almost impossible to use without cheating.

So I developed Galac6 to offer an alternative designed for students: an AI that helps understand, revise and progress — without doing homework for them. Galac6 leverages the best AI models on the market (Anthropic, Google, Mistral), used where each one excels, to guarantee relevant and educational responses.

Raphael Haik, Paris, France
