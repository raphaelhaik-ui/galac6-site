About Galac6 (galac6.io)

Updated on 9 November 2026

Engineer, entrepreneur, and above all father of 3 children, I created Galac6 from a simple observation: homework takes up a huge part of family life — and it is a source of stress, both for children and parents.

Like you, I saw the temptation of ChatGPT: fast, available at all hours, capable of explaining everything. But also ill-suited to school, and almost impossible to use without cheating.

So I developed Galac6 to offer an alternative designed for students: an AI that helps understand, revise and progress — without doing homework for them. Galac6 leverages the best AI models on the market (Anthropic, Google, Mistral), used where each one excels, to guarantee relevant and educational responses.

Today, Galac6 goes further: it redefines learning at school, starting from what each pupil knows. Every exercise shows what the pupil gets right and what they still need to work on, topic by topic. Teachers save time, know each of their pupils and offer them work at their level. Pupils look for the answer themselves, and their parents understand how to help.

We are starting with French schools, in France and abroad. It is a first step.

Raphaël Haïk, founder of Galac6, Paris

Our mission

Galac6 (galac6.io) prepares each pupil for their class test in a unique way. The pupil learns the lesson on a Galac6 revision sheet. An AI then quizzes them to measure what they have really remembered. Next, Galac6 offers them exercises suited to their level. Throughout this work, a teaching chat helps them without ever giving them the answer. Each pupil can also practise dictation, with over 2,000 texts, and mental maths, and work on past brevet and bac papers. Teachers stay in charge of their choices: they see where their class stands and where each pupil stands. Parents follow their child's progress, strengths and what they still need to work on.

Our approach: guiding without giving the answer

Galac6 does not do the homework in the pupil's place. The tutor asks questions, gives hints and explains mistakes, so that the pupil finds the answer themselves. This is what sets it apart from general-purpose AIs, which hand out ready-made solutions.

All content follows the French curriculum, from CP to Terminale: homework help, dictations read aloud and marked, mental maths, quizzes, revision sheets, mock brevet and bac exams. A practice paper created by the AI is always presented as such, never as an official past paper.

Each step is explained on the page Galac6's teaching method.

Galac6 in figures

  • Over 2,000 dictations read aloud and marked mistake by mistake, from CP to lycée.
  • Over 400 official past exam papers (433 to date), including 129 brevet papers and 75 French bac papers.
  • Twelve school years, from CP to Terminale.

How much does Galac6 cost?

Galac6 is a paid service. The Discovery plan, at €0, gives access to every feature in limited amounts, each month. The Student plan costs €69 a year. For schools, pricing is on quote, with a free 3-month pilot on 3 classes. Details are on the Pricing page.

Pupils' data

Data is hosted in France, in Paris, and Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in Europe. It is never sold or used to train AIs. For pupils under 15, a parent's consent is requested by email. Everything is detailed on the Pupil data page (in French).
