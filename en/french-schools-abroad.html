Frequently asked questions

Your questions

Do we need to adapt Galac6 to our school?

No. Galac6 follows the official French curriculum, from CP to Terminale, the same as in your school. Your teachers find the topics, levels and exams they already know.

How do we add our pupils?

You import your pupil list from a file: an export from Pronote or École Directe, or a simple pasted list. No need to enter them one by one.

Not all our families read French. What can we do?

Emails to families are sent in French or in English, as your school chooses, and at your school's local time.

Where is pupil data hosted?

Data is hosted in France, in Paris, and Galac6's main artificial intelligence is processed in Europe. Only the pupil's teachers and their school's leadership can see their work. Full details are on the pupil data page (in French).

How much does Galac6 cost?

The pilot is free for 3 months for 3 classes, with no commitment. After that, we send you a quote in euros based on the number of your pupils and the modules chosen.
