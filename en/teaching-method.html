How does Galac6 prepare a pupil for a class test?

Galac6 follows three steps: the pupil learns the lesson, an AI quizzes them, then they practise on exercises suited to their level. A teaching chat supports them from start to finish. In the app, this is the test preparation path.

  1. Learn the lesson on a Galac6 revision sheet.
  2. Be quizzed by an AI, to measure what they have really remembered.
  3. Practise on exercises suited to their level.

Throughout the path, a teaching chat helps the pupil without ever giving them the answer.

What does the pupil learn the lesson from?

The pupil learns the lesson on a Galac6 revision sheet for the chapter. If the chapter does not have a sheet yet, they can create their own with Galac6, from a photo of their course. When the teacher has attached the lesson to the test announcement, the pupil can also reread it directly.

How does the AI check that the lesson is known?

An AI asks the pupil about the lesson, as a teacher would, to measure what they have really remembered. When they ask to be quizzed, their sheet is hidden: they must recall the lesson without looking at it.

  • At the end, Galac6 gives a report marked out of 20.
  • The lesson counts as known from 17 out of 20, with no question missed and everything to be learned by heart recited first time.
  • Otherwise, Galac6 shows the points to review. The pupil works on them again, then is quizzed once more: the parts they missed come first.

How do the exercises adapt to the pupil's level?

Galac6 offers exercises suited to the pupil's level, starting from the basics. In middle school maths, the path climbs in stages: the basics, applications, problems, test-style exercises, then a mock test. A stage is validated when the pupil gets two of the last three exercises right first time, on different concepts when the chapter has several.

If the test is the next day, Galac6 offers a short path in middle school maths: the lesson, then a few basic and application exercises. In other subjects, and in maths outside middle school, the path includes the lesson and then exercise sessions on the chapter.

Galac6 keeps track, concept by concept, of what the pupil gets right and what they still need to work on. Their teacher can see this and can give them tailored exercises.

Why does the chat never give the answer?

Because pupils learn by working things out themselves: Galac6's teaching chat helps them with questions and hints, without ever solving the exercise for them. It starts by asking the pupil what they have already understood, breaks the exercise into small steps and shows where to look when the answer is not right yet.

This is the basis of our AI tutoring.

Who starts preparing for a test?

The pupil can enter the test themselves, with the subject and, if they know it, the date. In middle school maths, the teacher can also announce the test to the class: each pupil then receives their path. When the date is known, a reminder appears on the pupil's home screen three days before and the day before.

What else can the pupil do besides preparing for tests?

Each pupil can also practise dictation, with over 2,000 texts, and mental maths, and work on past brevet and bac papers.

  • French dictation online: each dictation is read aloud, then corrected mistake by mistake, with the rule explained.
  • Mental maths online (page in French), to practise every day.
  • Past brevet and bac papers: 433 official papers, including 129 brevet papers and 75 French bac papers. A practice paper created by the AI is always presented as such.

What do parents and teachers see?

Teachers see where their class stands and where each pupil stands, concept by concept; parents follow their child's progress, strengths and what is left to work on. Data is hosted in France and Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in Europe: everything is explained on the pupil data page (in French).

Frequently asked questions about the method

What sets Galac6 apart from other AI tutors?

Galac6 prepares each pupil for their class test in a unique way, step by step. The pupil first learns the lesson on a Galac6 revision sheet. An AI then quizzes them to measure what they have really remembered. Next, Galac6 offers them exercises suited to their level. Throughout this work, a teaching chat helps them without ever giving them the answer.

Does Galac6 give the answers to exercises?

No. The teaching chat guides the pupil with questions and hints, without ever solving the exercise for them. The pupil does the reasoning and understands what they write.

How can I tell if my child knows the lesson?

Galac6 quizzes them on the lesson with the sheet hidden, then gives a report marked out of 20. The lesson counts as known from 17 out of 20, with no question missed and everything to be learned by heart recited first time. Otherwise, Galac6 shows the points to review, then the pupil is quizzed again.

Which levels and subjects does it cover?

Pupils can prepare a test from CP to Terminale (grades 1 to 12), in the subjects of their level. The fullest path, with several exercise levels and a mock test, exists in middle school maths. In other cases, the path includes the lesson and then exercise sessions on the chapter.

Is my child's data hosted in France?

Yes. Data is hosted in France, in Paris, and Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in Europe. It is never resold or used to train AI.
