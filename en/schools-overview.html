Giving teachers back
time for every pupil.

In England, every week27 hoursoutside lessons, on top of 23 h of teaching: planning, marking, tracking pupils and more.*

The problem

That is where a teacher's time goes, outside the classroom. Too little is left for intervention and differentiation: the struggling pupil waits their turn, and the strong pupil gets bored.

Galac6 takes on what takes the time, gives teachers back control of their teaching and gives pupils AI tools that help them succeed.

For teachers

  • Plan

    The Galac6 assistant helps you improve your own lessons, then builds homework and tests.

  • Mark

    Paper or online work: Galac6 suggests the mark and explains each mistake; you decide.

  • Track

    Every pupil, topic by topic: who is falling behind, who is improving, what is known and what is not.

  • Differentiate

    Tailored homework for each pupil, checked before sending, and group work you can follow.

For pupils

  • Get help

    A Socratic chat: Galac6 guides pupils with questions, without giving them the answer.

  • Learn the lesson

    Galac6 quizzes pupils and shows them what they still need to learn, not just reread.

  • Prepare for tests

    A pathway up to test day, built on the teacher's lessons: lesson, exercises, mock test.

  • Practise, sit a mock exam

    Over 2,000 French dictations, mental maths, over 400 official past papers (brevet and bac).

An example: intervention in three days

  1. MondayA test on paper: Galac6 marks the photographed scripts, you approve the marks.
  2. TuesdayThe class map shows who needs to revisit the topic. You know what to reteach.
  3. WednesdayTailored homework for each pupil: work on weak points or build on strengths. Checked by you.
