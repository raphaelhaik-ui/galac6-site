23 hof a full-time teacher's 50-hour week in England are spent teaching pupils.*

The problem

The rest? Planning lessons, homework and tests, then marking them. Almost no time is left for intervention and differentiation: the pupil who is falling behind and the one who is bored both wait their turn.

Galac6 takes on what takes the time, gives teachers back control of their teaching and gives pupils AI tools that make them do the work.

For teachers

  • Plan

    Galac6 starts from your own lessons to build homework and tests. Your lessons stay yours.

  • Mark

    Paper or online work: Galac6 suggests the mark and explains each mistake; you decide.

  • Track

    Every pupil, topic by topic: who is falling behind, who is improving, what is known and what is not.

  • Differentiate

    Tailored homework for each pupil, checked before sending, and group work you can follow.

For pupils

  • Learn the lesson

    Galac6 quizzes pupils and shows them what they still need to learn, not just reread.

  • Prepare for tests

    A pathway up to test day, built on the teacher's lessons: lesson, exercises, mock test.

  • Practise

    Over 2,000 French dictations read aloud and marked, mental maths at each pupil's level.

  • Sit a mock exam

    Over 400 official French past papers (brevet and bac), timed, with detailed marking.

An example: intervention in three days

  1. MondayA test on paper. Galac6 marks the photographed scripts, you approve the marks.
  3. TuesdayThe class map shows who needs to revisit the topic. You know what to reteach.
  5. WednesdayEach pupil gets tailored homework on their weak points, checked by you before sending.
