For the teacher

Time saved

Galac6 helps with planning and marking, 27 h a week outside lessons in England*, and gives time back for intervention and differentiation.

For teachers

  • Share your lessons

    Your pupils find them in Galac6: at home, the AI helps them and explains your lessons.

  • Plan

    Creation assistants help you improve lessons and exercises, and turn them into homework and tests.

  • Mark

    Paper or online work: Galac6 suggests the mark and explains each mistake; you decide.

  • Intervention and differentiation

    Every pupil tracked topic by topic, and tailored homework for each, checked by you before sending.

For pupils, at home

  • Get help

    A Socratic chat that draws on the teacher's lessons and guides pupils with questions.

  • Learn the lesson

    Galac6 quizzes pupils on their teacher's lesson and shows what they still need to learn.

  • Prepare for tests

    A pathway up to test day, built on the teacher's lessons: lesson, exercises, mock test.

  • Practise, sit a mock exam

    Over 2,000 French dictations, mental maths, over 400 official past papers (brevet and bac).

An example: intervention in three days

  1. MondayA test on paper: Galac6 marks the photographed scripts, you approve the marks.
  2. TuesdayThe class map shows who needs to revisit the topic. You know what to reteach.
  3. WednesdayTailored homework for each pupil: work on weak points or build on strengths. Checked by you.
