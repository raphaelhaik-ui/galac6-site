How it works

A dictation in four steps

  1. The levelYour child picks their class: Galac6 offers a suitable dictation, from CP to lycée.
  2. ListeningGalac6 reads the text aloud, as a parent or teacher would.
  3. WritingYour child writes on screen, or on paper and then takes a photo of the page.
  4. MarkingEvery mistake is reviewed one by one, with the matching rule, so it is not made again.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions about dictation

Are the dictations really read aloud?

Yes. Galac6 reads every dictation aloud: your child listens and writes it down, without an adult having to dictate.

How is the dictation marked?

Galac6 compares the copy with the text, reviews every mistake one by one and explains the rule to apply: agreement, homophone, conjugation, spelling or punctuation.

For which levels?

From CP to 3e, with dictations written for every class, and up to Terminale with extracts from great public-domain authors. The dictations follow the French curriculum.

Can teachers give a dictation to their class?

Yes. Each pupil takes the dictation alone on screen, and Galac6 suggests the marking and the grade: the teacher has the final say. Paper copies can be scanned, then marked.

How much does online dictation cost?

The Discovery plan includes one dictation a month to try it. The Student plan (€69 a year or €7.99 a month) and the Family plan (€99 a year or €11.99 a month, up to 4 children) give unlimited dictations. For schools, pricing is on quote.
