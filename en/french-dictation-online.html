How it works

A dictation in four steps

  1. The levelYour child chooses the text, the level (easy, medium, hard or expert) and the length of the dictation.
  2. ListeningGalac6 reads the text sentence by sentence with a synthetic voice. The speed can be adjusted and each sentence can be played again.
  3. WritingYour child chooses to write on a keyboard or on paper. On paper, they then send a photo of their page.
  4. MarkingEach mistake is reviewed one by one, with the expected word, the rule explained and the mark out of 10.

On a keyboard or on paper

A paper dictation marked online, from a photo

Before starting, the pupil chooses how to write: on a keyboard or on paper. On paper, they write on their sheet while Galac6 reads, sentence by sentence.

At the end, they send their page as a photo, a PDF or a Word file, or by scanning it from their phone. If the photo is blurred or too small, Galac6 tells them and offers to take it again.

Galac6 reads the page, then the pupil corrects any words misread before the mark: their sheet is what counts. The marking is then the same as for a dictation done on a keyboard, mistake by mistake.

On a keyboard, a rereading step shows roughly how many mistakes remain, so the pupil can try to find them before submitting.

Reliable marking

How does the automatic marking work?

Galac6 compares the pupil's page with the dictation text, word by word. Each mistake is noted and the mark out of 10 follows the scale below: the same paper always gets the same mark.

There are three types of mistake. A grammar mistake (agreement, conjugation, homophone such as a or à, ce or se) takes off 0.75 points. A spelling mistake on a word takes off 0.5 points. A minor mistake (accent, capital letter, punctuation) takes off 0.25 points. Forms allowed by the 1990 French spelling reform are accepted.

Each mistake is explained in simple words.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions about dictation

Which app can I use for online dictations with automatic marking?

Galac6 is an online French dictation app for pupils from CP to Terminale. It reads the dictation aloud, marks each mistake one by one while explaining the rule, and gives a mark out of 10. It works in the browser, on a computer, tablet or phone.

Are the dictations really read aloud?

Yes. Galac6 reads each dictation sentence by sentence with a synthetic voice. The speed can be adjusted and the child can replay a sentence as many times as they wish, with no adult needed to dictate.

Is the automatic marking reliable?

The page is compared with the text word by word. Each mistake is noted and the mark out of 10 follows the scale, so the same paper always gets the same mark. Each mistake is explained with its rule.

Can a dictation written on paper be marked from a photo?

Yes. The pupil chooses to write on paper, writes while Galac6 reads, then sends their page as a photo or a PDF, or scans it from their phone. They correct any words Galac6 misread, then receive the marking mistake by mistake and their mark.

Which levels are the dictations for?

From CP to 3e, with dictations written for each class, and up to Terminale with extracts from great public-domain authors. In 3e, the dictations from past brevet papers are also offered.

Can the dictation given by the teacher be marked?

Yes. The child takes a photo of the dictation text, pastes it, or adds an image, a PDF or a Word file. Galac6 reads this text to them and then marks their page.

Can teachers give a dictation to their class?

Yes. Teachers create a dictation by pasting a text or taking a photo of a sheet, send it to the class with a deadline and choose the marking scale. Each pupil does the dictation alone on their screen, and paper copies can be uploaded as photos and then marked.

How much does online dictation cost?

Galac6 is a paid service. The Discovery plan, at €0 and limited, includes one dictation a month to try it, with no bank card. The Student plan (€69 a year or €7.99 a month) and the Family plan (€99 a year or €11.99 a month, for up to 4 children) give unlimited dictations. For schools, pricing is on quote.

Where is my child's data?

Data is hosted in France, in a database in Paris. Galac6 shows no advertising and does not resell any data. Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in Europe.
