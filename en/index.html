Frequently asked questions

Everything parents and students ask us, in full transparency.

Galac6 is for students from 1st to 12th grade and their parents. Whether your child needs a helping hand with homework, or is preparing for an important test, Galac6 is here to support them — at their own pace, in their subject, at their level.
No — and that's the whole point. Galac6 doesn't do homework for your child. It helps them ask the right questions, identify what they don't know yet, so they can find the answers themselves. The goal: build real confidence, not fake grades.
Never. Galac6 steps in where neither the teacher nor the parents can always be: at 9 PM, alone with homework. It's the caring companion that takes over when no one is available. For younger students, it can even become a great tool for parent-child interaction.
Galac6 uses AI to guide the student, not to think for them. The AI analyzes the student's level, gaps and learning pace, then offers tailored explanations, targeted review questions and personalized encouragement — in French, following the French National Education curriculum.
ChatGPT gives answers. Galac6 gives skills. ChatGPT isn't designed for children, doesn't know the French curriculum and has no educational logic. Galac6 is specifically designed for the French school system , with an approach that pushes students to truly learn — not to copy-paste.
15 to 30 minutes is enough for effective revision. Galac6 isn't designed to replace leisure — it's designed to optimize study time. A few targeted sessions per week already make a real difference in results.
Yes, and it's non-negotiable. Galac6 is designed in France, in line with the GDPR. Data is hosted in France and Galac6's artificial intelligence is processed in Europe; only a few technical services, such as hosting of the website, rely on providers located outside the European Union. We never sell your children's data to third parties. The privacy and security of minors' data are at the core of our design.
Yes, it is a priority. Data is hosted in France, in Paris, and Galac6's main artificial intelligence is processed in Europe. Each account only sees what it is allowed to see: at school, only the student's teachers and school leadership can access their work. Galac6 platform administration accounts are protected by two-factor authentication, and passwords known to have leaked are refused. Data is never sold or used to train AIs, and a deleted account is erased within 30 days. A few services remain outside the European Union, such as hosting of the website: everything is detailed on the Student data page.
Galac6 covers the main subjects of the French curriculum: French, Mathematics, History-Geography, Sciences, English and more — from 1st to 12th grade. Content automatically adapts to the student's level and grade.
Yes. Parents have access to a tracking dashboard to see subjects studied, progress made and areas to improve. At Galac6, we believe in the active role of parents in education — the tool is designed to strengthen that bond, not replace it.
