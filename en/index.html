The problem

General-purpose AIs do the work. Galac6 makes students work.

The AI that would rather teach the student than do it for them: any AI can do the homework, Galac6 teaches how to do it yourself.

How it works

Every answer helps the student progress, and informs those who support them.

  1. The student worksThey do their exercises, dictations and quizzes, and learn their lessons.
  2. Every answer is analysedGalac6 looks at the reasoning, not just the result.
  3. A mastery map is builtTopic by topic: mastered, in progress, to review.
  4. Galac6 offers tailored workTopics not yet mastered are worked on again until they are.
  5. Parents and teacher see where the student standsA clear report, without waiting for the next test.
  6. The teacher decidesThey set personalised homework based on what they see.
  7. Back to the studentAnd the loop starts again, more accurate with every round.
Who it is for

A tutor that teaches you how to work

Like a good tutor, Galac6 teaches the student how to work: getting organised, learning lessons, practising. It asks questions instead of giving the answer, and builds on what the student already knows. It corrects, explains mistakes and has each topic worked on again until it is mastered. It encourages at every step: struggling or ahead, every child gets the right challenge and gains confidence.
