Alternatives to Eliott: 5 AI tutors compared for your child

By Raphaël Haïk, founder of Galac6 · Published 9 November 2026 · 9 min read

Eliott is a French AI tutor, from 6e (grade 6, the first year of middle school) to higher education, often mentioned when people look for homework help through artificial intelligence. There are other options: Galac6 (galac6.io), Kartable with its AI private tutor Alfa, SchoolMouv and its AI coach, ChatGPT's study mode and Khanmigo. Here is what each one offers, according to their official pages consulted on 4 November 2026, and how to choose.

The key points in 30 seconds

  • Eliott guides the pupil without doing the work for them, from 6e to higher education, including the technological, vocational and agricultural tracks.
  • Galac6 goes from CP to Terminale (grades 1 to 12): a tutor that guides with questions, dictations read aloud, mental maths and mock exams on official papers of the brevet and the French bac. The brevet is the national exam at the end of middle school, and the baccalauréat (bac) the one at the end of lycée.
  • Kartable (Alfa) and SchoolMouv are based first on lessons written by teachers, with an AI assistant on top.
  • ChatGPT's study mode is a setting of a general-purpose assistant, from age 13. Khanmigo reserves its parents' subscription for families living in the United States.
  • To choose, look first at your child's age, then at how they work and the practice they need.

Why look for an alternative to Eliott?

Most often, because your child is in primary school: according to its website, Eliott starts in 6e. Other families are looking for specific practice or a different setting. Here are the most common reasons:

  • your child is in primary school, whereas Eliott starts in 6e;
  • you are looking for specific practice: dictations read aloud, mental maths, mock exams;
  • your child learns better with lessons and videos written by teachers;
  • you want data hosted in France; Eliott, for its part, states that it hosts data in the European Union;
  • your child's school or teacher already uses another tool.

What are the alternatives to Eliott in 2026?

The five closest alternatives are Galac6, Kartable with Alfa, SchoolMouv, ChatGPT's study mode and Khanmigo. They do not cover the same ages, as this diagram shows.

Galac6 (galac6.io)

An AI tutor designed around the French curriculum, from CP to Terminale. It guides the pupil with questions and hints, without writing the solution, and offers around the tutor dictations read aloud, mental maths, revision sheets from a photo of the lesson and mock exams on official papers. We come back to it below.

Kartable with Alfa

Kartable offers lessons, corrected exercises and revision sheets from CE1 (grade 2) to general Terminale. Its AI private tutor, Alfa, answers questions on lessons, creates quizzes from a photo of the lesson and guides the pupil step by step on a photographed exercise. Kartable states that Alfa is designed to guide the pupil without giving the answer directly, with a monthly usage limit. It is used on the web and as an app.

SchoolMouv

SchoolMouv covers CP to Terminale with lesson sheets, videos, quizzes, flashcards and brevet and bac past papers. Its plans include an AI coach by chat; the Premium plan adds live help from a teacher by chat, from 5 pm to 8 pm on weekdays and from 2 pm to 5 pm at weekends. The home page does not detail how the AI coach works.

ChatGPT's study mode

ChatGPT is a general-purpose assistant. Its study mode, included in all plans, asks questions instead of giving the answer directly. It has to be turned on, and OpenAI points out that it can make mistakes. OpenAI's terms of use for Europe require users to be at least 13, with a parent's permission before 18. Our article Galac6 or ChatGPT for homework (in French) details these differences.

Khanmigo

Khanmigo is Khan Academy's AI tutor. Its page states that it never gives the answer and that it supports learners from elementary school to university. However, the parents' subscription is reserved for people living in the United States, with an American billing address: a family in France therefore cannot subscribe today.

What criteria should you look at to choose an alternative to Eliott?

Look first at your child's level, then at how the tool helps them. Six criteria are enough to decide:

  1. Your child's level. A tool that starts in 6e will be of no use to a pupil in CE2 (grade 3). ChatGPT requires users to be at least 13.
  2. Guidance without giving the answer. A tutor that does the exercise for the pupil does not help them progress. Check that this guidance is permanent, not a setting to turn on. Our article Which AI for homework without giving the answers? (in French) details this point.
  3. The French curriculum of their class. The examples, vocabulary and methods should be those your child sees in class.
  4. The practice they need. Dictations, mental maths, revision sheets, mock brevet or bac exams: not all tools offer them.
  5. Follow-up for parents and teachers. Can you see what they have worked on? Can their teacher use it with the class?
  6. Price and data. A plan to try it, a clear price, and data hosting you know about.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Eliott?

It depends on your child. Galac6 (galac6.io) is suitable if your child is in primary school, or if you are looking for an AI tutor with dictations read aloud, mental maths and mock exams on official papers. Kartable with Alfa and SchoolMouv are suitable if your child learns with lessons, sheets or videos written by teachers. ChatGPT's study mode can be useful for an independent teenager over 13.

Is there an alternative to Eliott for primary school?

Yes. According to its website, Eliott starts in 6e. Galac6 and SchoolMouv cover primary school from CP, and Kartable from CE1. In primary school, Galac6 offers a tutor that guides with questions, dictations read aloud and mental maths.

Is Khanmigo available in France?

Not for families, according to Khanmigo's parents page consulted on 4 November 2026: the parents' subscription is reserved for people living in the United States, with an American billing address.

Can ChatGPT's study mode replace Eliott?

In part. Study mode guides the pupil with questions and is included in all ChatGPT plans, but it has to be turned on, OpenAI points out that it can make mistakes, and ChatGPT requires users to be at least 13, with a parent's permission before 18. It is not a tool designed around the curriculum of a class.

How much do Eliott and its alternatives cost?

As of 4 November 2026, Eliott lists €69.99 a year for one pupil. Galac6 offers the Discovery plan at €0, limited, then the Student plan at €69 a year or €7.99 a month. Kartable lists €14.99 a month on the monthly plan, Alfa included. SchoolMouv lists promotional prices from €4.99 a month. Study mode is included in all ChatGPT plans. Prices change: check them on each website.

Do you need to install an application to use Galac6?

No. Galac6 is used in the browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install.
