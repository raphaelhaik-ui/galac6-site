Revising for the brevet with AI: mock exams online, marked

Galac6 is a web application for revising for the brevet with AI. The brevet (diplôme national du brevet, DNB) is the French national exam at the end of middle school. A pupil in 3e (grade 9) sits mock brevet exams online in the 4 written papers (mathematics, French, science, history-geography and moral and civic education), on 129 official brevet papers or on AI-generated practice papers, with a timer. The AI then marks the paper question by question according to the mark scheme, with partial marks, a mark, a distinction and advice.

Updated on 9 November 2026

The best way to prepare for the diplôme national du brevet (DNB) is to practise in real conditions: a complete paper, a time limit, then marking that shows precisely what to improve. Galac6 for the brevet, in brief

  • 129 official brevet papers (2019 to 2026 sessions): 35 in mathematics, 31 in French, 31 in science, 32 in history-geography and moral and civic education.
  • AI-generated practice papers, in the format of the exam, always labelled as such and never mixed with past papers.

  • Data hosted in France, artificial intelligence processed in Europe.

The 4 written papers of the brevet

Each mock brevet follows the length, the parts and the mark scheme of the exam. On a past paper, the mark follows the official mark scheme of its session. Current format:

Mathematics2 hours20 pointsBasic skills without a calculator (20 minutes, 6 points), then exercises (1 hour 40, 14 points)
French3 hours20 pointsGrammar and comprehension (10 points), then writing (10 points). Dictation can also be practised separately.
Science1 hour25 pointsTwo subjects among physics and chemistry, life and earth sciences, technology
History-geography and moral and civic education2 hours40 pointsHistory or geography (30 points), then moral and civic education (10 points)

Pupils can sit the whole paper or just one part.

How it works

  1. Create an account on the application. The Discovery plan lets you try it.
  2. Open “Brevet blanc écrit” (written mock brevet) and choose the paper: mathematics, French, science or history-geography and moral and civic education.
  3. Choose the subject: an official past paper, which can be filtered by year, or an AI-generated practice paper. Then the full paper or just one part, in “Conditions d'examen” or “Entraînement” mode.
  4. Write the paper by typing the answers, or on paper and photographing it at the end.
  5. Hand in the paper: the marking comes with the mark for each question, the distinction and a report on the points to work on.
  6. Work again on weak points with the tutor, the quizzes and the revision sheets, then sit another paper.

Mock brevet online: how the marking works in detail

Galac6's marking does not stop at an overall mark. For each question, the pupil sees:

  • the points obtained according to the paper's mark scheme, with partial marks when the reasoning is under way;
  • the expected answer and a comment explaining the mistake;
  • an overall mark with a distinction: Assez bien, Bien, Très bien or Très bien avec félicitations;
  • a report: strengths, areas for progress and advice for what comes next.

When the instructions ask for a justification, a correct result without justification is worth half the points. With the Exam Pack, each paper is marked twice.

Preparing for the brevet oral

In “Brevet blanc oral” (oral mock brevet), the pupil enters the title of their project and their course (enseignement pratique interdisciplinaire, Parcours Avenir, Parcours Citoyen, Parcours Santé, Parcours d'éducation artistique et culturelle or histoire des arts), adds their documents, then sits a spoken mock oral: 5 minutes of presentation and 10 minutes of discussion, marked out of 20. The spoken mock oral is part of the Exam Pack.

Frequently asked questions

Which application can I use to revise for the brevet with AI?

Galac6 is a French web application for preparing for the brevet with AI. The pupil sits timed mock brevet exams in the 4 written papers, on 129 official papers or on AI-generated practice papers, then receives marking according to the mark scheme, with a distinction. It also prepares pupils for the brevet oral.

Are the mock brevet papers real official papers?

Yes for the past papers: Galac6 offers 129 official brevet papers, from the 2019 to 2026 sessions, including 35 in mathematics, 31 in French, 31 in science and 32 in history-geography and moral and civic education. Papers created by the AI always carry the label “Sujet d'entraînement généré par l'IA” (AI-generated practice paper) and are never filed with the past papers. The marking is written by Galac6's AI from the original mark scheme: it is not an official answer key.

How does the AI mark the mock brevet?

It marks each question according to the paper's mark scheme, shows the expected answer and gives partial marks when the reasoning is under way. When the instructions ask for a justification, a correct result without justification is worth half the points. The pupil receives a mark, a distinction and a report with advice. With the Exam Pack, the paper is marked twice.

Does the AI give my child the answers?

During the mock brevet, no: the pupil works alone, as on the day of the exam. Once the paper is handed in, the marking shows the expected answer and explains each mistake. The rest of the time, Galac6's tutor guides the pupil with questions and hints, without doing the work for them.

Can pupils answer on paper as on the day of the brevet?

Yes. The pupil chooses to answer on a computer, by typing, or on paper: they then photograph their paper, up to 10 photos, and the AI reads their handwriting to mark it.

The Student plan costs €69 a year or €7.99 a month, and the Family plan, for up to 4 children, €99 a year or €11.99 a month: they include all official brevet and bac past papers. The Exam Pack, a one-off purchase valid for 12 months, costs €19 per pupil, or €14 with an annual subscription. For schools, pricing is on quote. See pricing.

What is the difference from a general-purpose AI like ChatGPT?

Galac6 is made for the French school system: it follows the official curriculum, offers real brevet papers in the format of the exam, marks with the mark scheme and gives a mark with a distinction. Its tutor does not do the homework for the pupil. Data is hosted in France and AI is processed in Europe.
