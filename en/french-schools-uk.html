Frequently asked questions

Your questions

Does Galac6 follow the French curriculum, even in the United Kingdom?

Yes. Galac6 follows the official French curriculum, from CP to Terminale, the same as in your school. It does not follow any other country's curriculum. Your teachers find the topics, levels and exams they already know.

Do our teachers keep control of marking?

Yes. Galac6 suggests the correction and the mark, and the teacher has the final say. For quizzes and tests created with the help of AI, the teacher reviews and approves every question.

Not all our families read French. What can we do?

Emails to families are sent in French or in English, as your school chooses, and at your school's local time, not Paris time.

Can our pupils prepare for the brevet and the bac from the United Kingdom?

Yes. They practise on official brevet, French bac and maths bac past papers, including 112 set outside France, as mock exams with detailed marking. Galac6 does not handle exam registration.

Where is pupil data hosted?

Data is hosted in France, in Paris, and Galac6's main artificial intelligence is processed in Europe. Only the pupil's teachers and their school's leadership can see their work. Full details are on the pupil data page (in French).
