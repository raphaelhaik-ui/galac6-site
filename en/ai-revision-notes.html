A sheet based on the real lesson from class

A useful revision sheet covers what the teacher actually did in class. That is why Galac6 starts from the pupil's own lesson: the pupil photographs it, page by page, and Galac6 reads the photos to make a clear sheet suited to their level. This works with a printed lesson as well as with handwritten notes.

Galac6 works in the browser, on a phone, tablet or computer: the pupil takes the photos directly with their phone.

Three ways to create a sheet

“Ma vraie leçon” (my real lesson)

The pupil photographs their lesson, even handwritten: Galac6 turns it into a sheet.

Official curriculum

No lesson to hand? The pupil chooses a chapter of the curriculum, published on Éduscol, the French education ministry's website.

“Guide pour faire mes fiches” (guide to making my sheets)

Galac6 teaches the pupil to build their sheet themselves, with questions and hints.

With the guide, Galac6 follows a Socratic approach: it suggests a plan based on the photos, asks the pupil what they remember from each part, praises what is right and points them towards what is missing with a hint, without writing for them. The final sheet shows what the pupil found alone and what Galac6 added.

How it works

  1. The pupil opens Revision sheets from their home screen.
  2. They photograph their lesson, or choose a chapter of the official curriculum.
  3. They choose the format: detailed sheet or summary sheet.
  4. Galac6 creates the sheet, which the pupil reads through and can export as a PDF.
  5. They get quizzed with the button “Je suis prêt, interroge-moi !”: 5 to 7 questions, then a report out of 20.

Several formats, depending on the stage of revision

Detailed sheet

With examples and explanations, to understand the lesson.

Summary sheet

Definitions, formulas and properties, for last-minute reading.

Mind map

From a chapter of the curriculum, ideas linked to one another, to get an overview.

PDF export

To print the sheet or keep it on the phone.

From the sheet to the quiz

Reading a sheet again is not enough to know a lesson. On each sheet, the button “Je suis prêt, interroge-moi !” starts the quiz “Interroge-moi sur ma leçon” (quiz me on my lesson): Galac6 asks 5 to 7 questions on the lesson, in writing or aloud, then gives a report with a mark out of 20. When the pupil reopens their sheet, the parts to review are highlighted, and the next quiz starts with them.

In middle school, before a class test, “Préparer mon contrôle” (prepare my class test) goes further: the pupil enters the date, and Galac6 prepares a path in stages on this lesson, up to the day of the test. The stages of this preparation, from the sheet to the exercises, are presented on the page Galac6's teaching method.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general assistant: you can ask it to summarise a text, on any topic. Galac6 is designed for a pupil's revision, from the photo of the lesson through to the quiz.

A general assistant

  • Designed for all audiences and all topics.
  • Answers the request as it is asked.
  • Does not link to the class curriculum.

Galac6

  • Starts from the photo of the pupil's lesson, or from the official curriculum.
  • Can guide the pupil to build their sheet themselves.
  • Quizzes the pupil on their lesson and gives a report out of 20.
  • Data hosted in France, artificial intelligence processed in Europe.

From CP to Terminale, in every subject

Revision sheets are available at all levels, from primary school (in French) to lycée, in all the subjects covered by Galac6: French, mathematics, history and geography, sciences, languages, philosophy and the lycée specialities. Frequently asked questions from parents

Which platform can create revision sheets from my child's lessons?

Galac6 does this for pupils from CP to Terminale. The pupil takes a photo of their lesson, even handwritten, and Galac6 draws from it a clear revision sheet, suited to the pupil's level.

Does Galac6 make the sheet for the pupil?

The pupil has the choice. They can get a ready-made sheet from their lesson, or follow the “Guide pour faire mes fiches”: Galac6 suggests a plan, asks them what they remember from each part and guides them with questions and hints. The final sheet distinguishes what the pupil found alone from what Galac6 added. For a pupil to build it themselves, see also our method: how to make a revision sheet in 5 steps (in French).

What types of sheet are available?

A detailed sheet, with examples and explanations, or a summary sheet, with definitions, formulas and properties. Sheets can be exported as a PDF. From a chapter of the curriculum, Galac6 can also create a mind map.

What if my child does not have their lesson to hand?

They can choose a chapter of the official curriculum: Galac6 then creates the sheet from the curricula published on Éduscol, and the pupil can specify a point to explore further.

Does Galac6 help prepare for a class test from a sheet?

Yes, in middle school. With “Préparer mon contrôle”, the pupil enters the date of their test and Galac6 prepares a path in stages on this lesson, of which the quiz is the first stage.

What is the difference from ChatGPT for making sheets?

ChatGPT is a general assistant, for all audiences and all topics. Galac6 is designed for pupils: it starts from the photo of the lesson, follows the French national curriculum, can guide the pupil to build their sheet themselves, then quizzes them on their lesson. Its data is hosted in France.

How much do Galac6 revision sheets cost?
