Online tutoring: an AI tutor that helps with homework without giving the answers

Updated on 9 November 2026

Galac6 is an online tutoring service for pupils from CP to Terminale (grades 1 to 12): an AI tutor that helps with homework without giving the answers. It guides pupils with questions and hints, in every subject, following the French national curriculum. Galac6 works in the browser, on a phone, tablet or computer; data is hosted in France and artificial intelligence is processed in Europe.

Online tutoring from CP to Terminale

Online tutoring lets a pupil get help at home, at the moment they do their homework, with no appointment and no travel. With Galac6, that help comes from an AI tutor designed for the French school system: it knows the primary, middle school and lycée curricula, adapts to the pupil's level and is available in the evening as well as at the weekend.

Its role is not to do the homework, but to teach the pupil how to do it. Galac6 refuses to hand over a ready-made answer: it leads the pupil to find it, so they really understand and can do it again alone on the day of the test.

Homework help that does not give the answers

Instead of writing out the solution, the tutor asks questions, gives more and more precise hints and checks that the pupil has understood before moving on. The pupil stays active from start to finish.

  • It starts by asking the pupil what they have already understood.
  • It breaks the exercise into small steps and gives a lead at each step.
  • It praises what is right and shows where to look when something is not right yet.
  • When the pupil suggests a calculation result, Galac6 can check it with a calculation tool before telling them whether they are right.

How it works

  1. The pupil's account is created with their class. Outside school settings, for a child under 15, a parent gives consent to the account.
  2. The pupil asks the tutor a question, or takes a photo of the exercise they are stuck on or of their lesson.
  3. Galac6 guides them step by step, with questions and hints suited to their level, without giving a ready-made answer.
  4. The pupil practises and gets quizzed: exercises, quizzes, questions on their lesson with a report out of 20.
  5. You follow their progress, with progress reports in the Family plan, and so do their teachers if they use Galac6 with their school.

All subjects, including the lycée specialities

Galac6 covers the whole French school path, including primary school:

  • French: reading, grammar, conjugation, spelling, writing, commentary and essay.
  • Mathematics: arithmetic, fractions, geometry, algebra, up to the advanced mathematics option in Terminale.
  • History and geography, moral and civic education, life and earth sciences, physics and chemistry, English, Spanish, German, philosophy.
  • Lycée specialities: mathematics, physics and chemistry, life and earth sciences, economics and social sciences, computer science, history-geography-geopolitics, humanities and literature, English language and literature.

How is it different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general assistant: it answers all kinds of requests, for all audiences. Depending on how the question is asked, it may write out the solution to an exercise directly. Galac6 starts from a different need, that of a pupil who has to learn.

A general assistant

  • Designed for all audiences and all topics.
  • Answers the request as it is asked.
  • No link with the pupil's teachers.

Galac6

  • Follows the French national curriculum, from CP to Terminale.
  • Does not give the answer directly: it guides with questions and hints.
  • Parents follow progress, and so do teachers through the school.
  • Data hosted in France, artificial intelligence processed in Europe.
